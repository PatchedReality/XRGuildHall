Complete individual bookcase from floor/plinth to the top of its attached carved crown; excludes separate lettering, wall frieze and room cornice.

Visual proportional estimates, one representative full-case estimate per full-room image, equally weighted. These are not measurements of known physical dimensions. Pixel spans are approximate visual readings at 2048 px image width, not solved camera geometry.

Assumptions: seats 45–50 cm above the floor; armchair backs 85–100 cm overall; books 25–30 cm tall. Images without furniture/book anchors use an assumed 35–45 cm shelf pitch and have lower confidence. These are modeling assumptions, not dimensions supplied with the images.
